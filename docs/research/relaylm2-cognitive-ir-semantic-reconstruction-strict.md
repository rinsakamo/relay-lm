> frozen finite consumer reconstruct that payload differently from P4

> the exact same qualified strict-output interface and resource envelope?

This remains a mechanism-control experiment about semantic sufficiency versus
bounded consumer accessibility. It is not a formation-quality, downstream-use,
operational-efficacy, durable-correction, transfer, or architecture experiment.
