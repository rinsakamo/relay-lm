## Confirmatory inference

Exactly two confirmatory paired tests are allowed:

1. H1 T vs G — incremental typed-packaging effect after role-accessibility control.
2. H2 G vs L — downstream role-accessibility effect with typing absent.

Use two-sided exact McNemar/sign tests on 24 paired outcomes and Holm-Bonferroni across exactly H1/H2 at familywise alpha 0.05. T vs L is descriptive only. No equivalence margin is preregistered, so non-rejection is not equivalence or sufficiency.
