## Frozen first gate

E6-CE1 uses 24 fresh F_SHARED K3 families. Each family has four public source examples and three distinct held-out future probes under one unchanged latent rule. The rule has width four, identity permutation, modulus ten, and exactly three active additive offsets in 1..3. All generated source/query vectors obey the inherited no-wrap discipline.

The four downstream arms are:

- `R DIRECT_RETRIEVAL`: no formation call; project the public source packet again at each future use.

- `C SEMANTIC_CACHE`: form once with the inherited P3 future-reusable gist contract and reuse unchanged.
